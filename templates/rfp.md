# RFP qualification and response

**Purpose:** Support bid/no-bid and an evidence-grounded proposal. Retain the issued RFP revision,
amendments, clarification authority and stated submission conditions. This note is unreviewed.

## Decision requested
State the bid recommendation, accountable authority, requested response and agreed date.
Distinguish mandatory eligibility from desirable fit; an unknown mandatory item is not compliant.

## Obligation register
| RFP location and exact requirement | Response and evidence | Fit / gap / unknown | Exception or clarification | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Delivery and commercial case
Link scope, effort ranges, cost inputs, assumptions, dependencies and roadmap. Explain exclusions,
client responsibilities and estimation confidence. Do not invent references or certifications.

## Submission readiness
Record unresolved obligations, required approvals, version consistency and the recipient-safe
deliverable selection. Drafting a response does not authorize submission or external sharing.

## Graph links
Register the issued RFP and each addendum as `sources`. For each mandatory or scored obligation, create a `finding` that quotes it (obligation ID and clause in `location`) and a `requirement` that cites that finding; list both here in `findings` and `requirements`. Link clarifications as `questions`, bid `risks`, `assumptions` and `estimates`, and the response `deliverables`. A new addendum reopens the requirements it changes.
