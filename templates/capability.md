# Business capability

**Purpose:** Describe what the business must be able to do, independent of who does it or which system supports it.

## Definition
Describe the capability and its `level`: L1 for a domain such as Order to Cash, L2 for a capability such as Credit Management, L3 for a detailed capability. Use `parent` for the hierarchy.

## Maturity
Rate `maturity_current` and `maturity_target` from 1 (ad hoc) to 5 (optimized), with the evidence for each rating. Use `unknown` until supported.

## Support
List the `goals` it serves, the `organizations` that perform it and the `findings` that describe it. Systems that support it list it in their own `capabilities`.
