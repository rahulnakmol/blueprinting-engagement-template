# Deliverable

**Purpose:** Record one output prepared for an audience and what it is built from.

## Audience and purpose
Name the audience in `stakeholders`, the decision or action it supports, the `format` (md, docx, pptx, xlsx, pdf-document, pdf-deck or zip) and the `version`.

## Contents
List the `goals`, `processes`, `requirements`, `decisions`, `findings`, `estimates`, `assumptions` and `risks` it presents. Every material claim in the output should trace to one of these nodes.

## Review and release
Set `record` to the retained export or package record. Record its review, its sharing approval and where it was sent. Exporting a file is not delivery or permission to share.
