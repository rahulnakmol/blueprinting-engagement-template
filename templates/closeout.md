# Closeout and continuing commitments

**Purpose:** Record won, lost, paused or completed status without erasing history or open work.
State the status evidence and named authority. No automatic outreach or case-study publication.

## Outcome and limits
Compare observed outcomes with the agreed criteria. Separate measured benefit, forecast benefit
and unknown benefit. Record client feedback with attribution and permission boundaries.

## Open commitments
List unresolved decisions, receiving owners, follow-up actions and agreed dates. A closed
opportunity is not proof that its obligations were withdrawn or its client data can be deleted.

## Retrospective and retention
Record what to repeat/change, supporting evidence and contextual limits. Review proposed
generalized lessons for cross-client reuse. Record the agreed retention/archive review and
who may authorize it; preserve material needed for outstanding commitments.
