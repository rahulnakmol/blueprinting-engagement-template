# Goal

**Purpose:** Record one business driver, objective, outcome or KPI the engagement must serve. Requirements and decisions trace back to goals.

## Statement
State the goal in business terms. Use `parent` to place an objective under its driver or a KPI under its outcome.

## Measure
Record the `metric`, current `baseline`, `target` and `target_date`. Leave unknown values empty and open a question; do not invent a baseline or target.

## Ownership and evidence
Name the accountable `owner` and the `stakeholders` who care. Link the `findings` and `sources` that establish the goal. A goal stated only by the consultant is a hypothesis until a stakeholder confirms it.
