# Data object

**Purpose:** Record a business data entity such as a customer, item, order or invoice, its owner and where it is kept.

## Definition
Set `data_type` and describe the entity, its key attributes and its business meaning. Name the accountable data `owner`.

## Location and quality
List the `systems` that hold it, marking the system of record, and the `organizations` that create or use it. Record known quality issues with their `findings`.

## Sensitivity
Set `sensitivity` and note any personal, financial or regulated content and the handling it requires.
