# Engagement debrief

**Purpose:** Preserve learning and prepare the next decision. State the actual covered period
and whether this is daily, weekly, agreed fortnightly, monthly or milestone-level reporting.
These views have different purposes; one brief status line cannot replace the full daily debrief.

## What changed
Link meeting/working notes and exact evidence revisions. Preserve context, outcomes, surprises,
competing accounts and the rationale for recommendations. Distinguish facts from interpretation.

## What remains uncertain
Record missing evidence, stale conclusions, unresolved decisions and response coverage gaps.
Name the action and accountable role; retain the original age of open decisions.

## Next response and lessons
State due responses under the agreed working calendar and zone. Report failed/uncertain saves
and missed checks honestly. This vault is the complete record; keep full notes and debriefs here.
If a Notion destination is separately configured, publish only permitted summaries and complete
debriefs to it after the required review.
Generalized lessons need contextual and permission review before cross-engagement reuse.
