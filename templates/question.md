# Open question

**Purpose:** Make one gap visible and owned: missing evidence, a clarification, a contradiction to resolve or a decision still needed.

## Question
State the exact question, its `question_type` and why the answer matters. List the nodes it blocks or changes in `affects`.

## Who can answer
List the `stakeholders` who can answer, the `owner` who will obtain the answer and the agreed `due` date.

## Answer
List the evidence that raised the question in `findings`. When it is answered, create a `finding` for the answer that lists this question in its `questions`, then set `status: closed`. An unanswered question stays open.
