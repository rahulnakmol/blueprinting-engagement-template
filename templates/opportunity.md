# Opportunity assessment

**Purpose:** Decide whether to pursue, pause or decline. Use for positioning and pre-sales;
use the separate RFP record for issued procurement obligations. This note is unreviewed.

## Decision requested
State the client's problem, business driver, desired outcome, sponsor and decision authority.
Record the requested response and agreed date; do not invent either.

## Evidence and positioning
Separate supplied facts from value hypotheses. Link exact sources and capability evidence.
Explain why the proposed approach fits; state material limitations, alternatives and what
would change the recommendation. Include stakeholder concerns and incentives without profiling.

## Commercial boundaries
Record scope/exclusions, dependencies, client effort, rate provenance, effort ranges and
confidence. Link reproducible estimate inputs; no implied fixed price, tax or benefit promise.

## Follow-through
Record qualification gaps, the next workshop/questions and owned next actions. Preserve history
if won, lost or paused. A pitch is not a client commitment or permission to contact them.

## Graph links
Link sponsors and buyers in `stakeholders`, the client entities in `organizations`, business drivers in `goals`, supporting findings and sources, pursuit risks, the effort `estimates` and the proposal `deliverables`. Create those nodes rather than describing them only here.
