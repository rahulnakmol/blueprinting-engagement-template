# Estimate

**Purpose:** Record one effort estimate as a range with its inputs and assumptions. An estimate is not a quote or a commitment.

## Range
Record the `unit` (for example person-days), the `low`, `likely` and `high` values and the `confidence`. Set `record` to the retained estimate record produced by Blueprinting's estimate helper.

## Basis
List the `requirements` estimated, the `assumptions` and `risks` that drive the range and the `sources` of rates or benchmarks. Effort is not elapsed time, and cost is not price.
