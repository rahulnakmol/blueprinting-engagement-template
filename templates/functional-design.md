# Functional design

**Purpose:** Decide how work should operate to meet the business outcome. Cover business
behavior, not implementation architecture. This is a proposal until qualified and confirmed.

## Business outcome and options
State drivers, measurable outcomes, scope, current constraints, recommendation and alternatives.
Link supporting and competing evidence. Explain trade-offs for sponsors and operating roles.

## As-is to to-be
Describe trigger, normal flow, decision points, exceptions, failure/recovery and end state.
Map actors, responsibilities, concerns, receiving handoffs and operating-model changes.

## Readiness and agreement
Track sub-mission findings, unresolved parent criteria, assumptions and missing evidence.
Specify functional UAT examples that distinguish correct from plausibly wrong outcomes.
Written confirmation must cover the exact version and scope from the named client authority.
A completed diagram, green checklist or source hash is not functional acceptance.
