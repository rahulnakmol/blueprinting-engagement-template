# Finding

**Purpose:** Capture one attributed claim from a source. One claim per note, so each can be verified, contradicted or reused independently.

## Claim
Quote or closely paraphrase the claim. Mark `claim_type` as a statement (someone said it), an observation (someone saw it) or an interpretation (the consultant inferred it). Keep original quotations unchanged.

## Attribution
List the `sources` and, in `location`, the exact page, section or timecode. Name the `speaker` as given, or `unknown`; list identified people in `stakeholders`.

## Verification
Set `evidence` to unverified, source-checked, clarified or contested. When accounts disagree, set `contested`, list the competing earlier findings in `contradicts` and open a question. Do not choose the more senior speaker as truth. If this finding answers open questions, list them in `questions`. Set `record` to the retained claim record when the claim is retained.
