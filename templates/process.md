# Process

**Purpose:** Describe one business process or step as it is today (as-is) or as proposed (to-be).

## Flow
State the `trigger`, the normal flow, decision points and the end `outcome`. Use `parent` to nest a step under its process.

## Exceptions and handoffs
Describe each exception, failure and recovery path, and every handoff between roles, teams or systems, including the receiving owner.

## Responsibilities
List performing `roles` and `organizations`, supporting `systems`, the `data` objects created or used, and the `capabilities` and `goals` served. For a to-be process, list the as-is processes it changes in `replaces`.

## Pain points and evidence
Record observed problems, volumes and timings with their `findings` and `sources`. Mark estimates as estimates.
