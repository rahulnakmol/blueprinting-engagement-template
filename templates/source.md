# Source

**Purpose:** Register one piece of supplied evidence so findings can cite it. Retain the material itself through Blueprinting intake first; this note points to it and does not replace it.

## Identity
State the source type, who supplied or produced it, the capture date and its exact location (file path, URL, meeting or page). Set `record` to the retained source record as `<record-id>@<revision>`, exactly as Blueprinting reports it. Name the participants in `stakeholders` and the client entities in `organizations`.

## Processing restrictions
Record consent, permitted processing, storage, retention and sharing limits. Unknown restrictions block processing; they are not permission.

## Quality and gaps
Note speaker uncertainty, missing sections, audio quality, translation, provisional machine transcription and anything that limits how far findings can rely on it.
