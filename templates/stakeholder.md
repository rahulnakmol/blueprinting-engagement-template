# Stakeholder

**Purpose:** Record a person or group whose decisions or work affect the engagement. Record business role, authority and stated concerns only; never record personal characteristics, opinions of the person or speculation about motives.

## Role and authority
State their `title`, the `organizations` and `roles` they belong to, and in `authority` what they may decide, approve or block. Unknown authority is a gap. When their authority is confirmed in writing, set `record` to the retained authority record.

## Interests and concerns
Record what they need from the outcome and the concerns they have stated, with the source. Rate the `influence` and `interest` of their role on the engagement, with the basis for each rating. Ratings are internal working notes: keep them out of client deliverables, and remember that people can ask to see what is recorded about them. Follow the engagement's retention policy.

## Engagement
Note agreed communication, open commitments to them and unresolved disagreements, without speculating about motives.
