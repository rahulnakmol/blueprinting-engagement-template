# Workshop and evidence record

**Purpose:** Resolve a named business uncertainty. Use before and after the workshop; do not
require live AI interaction. This note is working evidence, not an accepted decision.

## Preparation
State the outcome, participants/roles, decision authority and questions by unresolved issue.
Prepare an agenda, consultant checklist and client questionnaire only where needed.
Confirm recording consent, permitted processing, storage and retention before handling audio.

## Competing accounts and follow-up

authority who can decide, the exact question, receiving owner and next-working-day response.
Keep rich notes; derive a full daily debrief and permitted summary without discarding detail.
