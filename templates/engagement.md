# Engagement charter

**Purpose:** The single charter note for this engagement. Create exactly one; it is the place to start reading.

## Client and scope
Name the client, the business problem, the journey, in-scope and out-of-scope areas, and the start and expected end dates.

## People and authority
List sponsors, decision authorities and key contacts in `stakeholders`, client entities and partners in `organizations`, and the top-level business drivers in `goals`. Record who may confirm business decisions in writing. Unknown authority is a gap.

## Operating rules
Summarize the agreed processing, storage, retention, sharing, working calendar, time zone and reporting cadence from the retained engagement policy. Note what is still unknown.

## Current state
State the next decision, its audience and date, and the main open questions. Update this section as the engagement progresses; keep earlier states in Git history.
