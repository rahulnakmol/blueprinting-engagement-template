# Decision and action brief

**Purpose and disposition:** Record one item that needs a named authority's response. Identify whether it is for decision, action, review or information. Do not invent a decision request for an information-only item. Tasks the team will do itself are `action` notes, not decisions.

## Requested response
State the precise question, recommendation, accountable role, approval status and agreed `due` date. Separate the response deadline from the age of the unresolved decision.

## Basis and implications
Link the `goals`, `processes`, `requirements`, `findings`, `risks` and `assumptions` the item rests on, and the `questions` it resolves. Explain alternatives, competing accounts, trade-offs, dependencies and consequences of delay. State what remains uncertain.

## Authority and response
List the deciding `stakeholders`. Record the named client's authority and written reply covering this exact version. Silence, workshop notes and consultant recaps cannot establish approval. Retain earlier replies if stale. A sent follow-up satisfies a response obligation only, not the underlying decision. Set `record` to the retained decision record only after Blueprinting's `baseline` check passes; that, not this note's `status`, makes the listed requirements agreed.

## Next actions
Create an `action` note for each follow-up, with its accepting owner, dependency and agreed timing. Client contact, external sharing and changes to approved business decisions require separate review.
