# Decision and action brief

**Purpose and disposition:** Identify whether this item is for decision, action, review or
information. Do not invent a decision request for an information-only item.

## Requested response
State the precise question, recommendation, accountable role, approval status and agreed date.
Separate the response deadline from the age of the unresolved decision.

## Basis and implications
Link exact evidence and baseline scope/version. Explain alternatives, competing accounts,
trade-offs, dependencies and consequences of delay. State what remains uncertain.

## Authority and response
Record the named client's authority and written reply covering this exact version. Silence,
workshop notes and consultant recaps cannot establish approval. Retain earlier replies if stale.
A sent follow-up satisfies a response obligation only, not the underlying decision.

## Next actions
Name each action, accepting owner, dependency and agreed timing. Client contact, external
sharing and changes to approved business decisions require separate review.
